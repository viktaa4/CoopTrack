Features

Everything your cooperative needs in one place

Eight connected modules that work together, so every record checks the next.

01

Farmer Registration

Register each member once with phone number and main produce. Search and update any time.

02

Delivery Recording

Log produce, quantity, date and receipt number at the collection point, then verify.

03

Payment Management

Link every payment to a delivery and receipt so the right farmer gets the right amount.

04

Stock Management

See stock received, dispatched and available, with mismatches shown early.

05

Error Tracking

Wrong details, receipt mismatches and late records listed for staff to correct.

06

Reports

Farmer, delivery, payment and error reports for managers and committees.

07

Notifications

Why CoopTrack

Built for African farming cooperatives

Many cooperatives still rely on paper books and scattered spreadsheets. That leads to lost records, payment mistakes and late reports. CoopTrack replaces them with one secure, simple system.

Correct farmer records

Every delivery is linked to the right farmer.

Verified deliveries and payments

Receipts, deliveries and payments are matched automatically.

Reliable stock figures

Stock updates as produce is received and dispatched.

What makes it different

It does not only store records. It checks them.

CoopTrack compares deliveries, receipts, payments and stock, and tells staff when something does not match, so errors are fixed the same day.

  • Wrong farmer information
  • Receipt and payment mismatches
  • Stock that does not add up
  • Late delivery records
